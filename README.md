# Hi, I'm Chris

I build software for making music and moving with purpose. My current focus is Saine, Nota, and RISN.

## What I'm building

### [Saine](https://www.saine.studio/)
A music creation studio that runs in your browser. Arrange audio and MIDI, shape sounds with built-in instruments and effects, and make music alongside AI assistants through WebMCP.

[Try the studio](https://www.saine.studio/studio/)

### [Nota](https://github.com/nota-daw/nota)
A free, open-source music studio for macOS, Windows, and Linux. Arrangement, clip launching, modular modulation, and plugin support — with no subscription or account required.

[Explore Nota](https://nota-daw.github.io/nota-site/) · [Source code](https://github.com/nota-daw/nota)

### [RISN](https://risn.app/)
A training app for strength, running, and walking. Plans built around your week, progression that helps you decide what comes next, and clubs to keep you showing up. Currently in testing on iPhone.

[Join early access](https://risn.app/)
